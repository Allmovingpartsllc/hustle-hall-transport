Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know before requesting a ride or local delivery.

Book a ride Start a delivery

How is a ride estimate calculated?

Ride estimates begin at $5.00, plus $0.95 per mile for the first 20 miles, $0.85 for each mile after 20 miles, and $0.15 per minute. Tolls, added stops, or route changes can affect the final total.

How are package deliveries priced?

Small packages start at $5, medium at $10, large at $20, and Extra Large deliveries start at $50. Deliveries over 20 miles add a $10 distance surcharge plus $0.35 per mile after the first 20 miles.

When is payment due?

All payments are upfront. Select online payment when available or Pay in Person if it is offered with your request. Multi-day services are reviewed and confirmed before payment is taken.

Can I use a promo code?

Enter one promo code before submitting your request. Promo codes require an eligible order of at least $15. SCHOOL is for eligible rides and is limited to 30% off, up to $5 per ride. TKA is 45% off eligible orders, including multi-day ride estimates.

Can I request rides on more than one day?

Yes. Choose “Multi-day ride request” on the ride form, add your service dates and schedule details, and you will see a pre-approval estimate before it is reviewed.

How do I track an order?

Use the Track Order page with your order number or the phone number used when booking. You can also check your customer portal after signing in.

What items cannot be delivered?

Hustle Hall Transport cannot accept illegal, dangerous, unsafe, or prohibited items. Let us know about fragile, perishable, or unusually heavy items so we can confirm whether the service can be completed safely.

Need help with a request?

Call or text 239-800-1380, or use the contact page to schedule a business call. We will confirm service details before the job is accepted.
